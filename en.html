ProDoc KZ: criminal procedure documents in Kazakhstan

A free tool that helps you draft typical documents for criminal cases in the Republic of Kazakhstan: fill in a form, check the text and download a Word file. The forms and the generated documents are in Russian and Kazakh. This page explains the service in English.

Student project. The authors are not lawyers or advocates. A template is not legal advice. Before you file a real document, have a lawyer or a legal clinic check it.

Why the forms are not in English

Under Article 30 of the Criminal Procedure Code of Kazakhstan (CPC), criminal proceedings are conducted in Kazakh, and Russian is officially used alongside it. A participant who does not speak the language of the proceedings may make statements, give testimony, file motions and complaints and read the case file in a language they know, and an interpreter is provided free of charge. This site does not generate documents in English, because an English text would still need translation before it could be filed. If you need to understand a generated document, show it to a lawyer or use a qualified translator.

Documents you can draft

Each document has its own page with an explanation: what it is for, who may file it, when and where. Open the page in Russian or Kazakh to fill in the form.

  • Motion to attach evidence to the case file (CPC art. 99, 259, 261): RU / KK
  • Motion to examine a witness (art. 99, 208): RU / KK
  • Motion to examine a specialist (art. 99): RU / KK
  • Motion to restore a missed deadline (art. 48-50): RU / KK
  • Complaint against a procedural decision, action or inaction (art. 100, 102, 105, 106): RU / KK
  • Complaint against the refusal of a motion (art. 99, 100, 105, 106): RU / KK
  • Statement withdrawing a complaint (art. 100): RU / KK
  • Statement about a criminal offence (art. 179, 180, 181): RU / KK

Deadlines under the CPC

ActionTime limitArticle
Deciding a motionImmediately; if that is impossible, no later than three days from the day the motion is made99
Complaint against acts and decisions during the investigation and trialMay be filed throughout the pre-trial investigation and the trial102
Complaint against termination of a case at the pre-trial stageWithin one year102
Review of a complaint by the head of the investigative unit or the prosecutor7 days; 3 days for some matters; up to 15 days in exceptional cases105
Complaint to the court on matters listed in art. 10615 days: from the day you read the decision, or from receiving the prosecutor's refusal, or from the end of 15 days after a complaint to the prosecutor went unanswered106

How time limits are counted (art. 48): the day on which a period begins is not counted; a period in days ends at 24:00 of the last day; if the last day is not a working day, the period ends on the next working day. Under art. 49 a deadline is met if the document is handed to the post office before it expires (the postmark decides). A deadline missed for a valid reason may be restored on a motion (art. 50). There is a deadline calculator in Russian and Kazakh. It does not take public holidays into account.

Who can file what

Motions under art. 99 can be filed by participants in the proceedings for themselves or for the person they represent. Complaints under art. 100 can be filed by participants and by individuals and legal entities whose interests are affected. Any individual can file a statement about a criminal offence (art. 180, 181); the applicant is warned about liability for a knowingly false report, and an anonymous report cannot be the basis for starting an investigation.

Participants' rights

The Russian and Kazakh pages "Rights of participants" (RU / KK) give a short summary of the rights of a suspect (art. 64), an accused (art. 65), a victim (art. 71) and a witness (art. 78), and of what a summons must contain (art. 208). In short, a suspect may have a defence counsel, may refuse to testify and may use an interpreter free of charge; a witness may refuse to give testimony that could expose themselves or close relatives to prosecution.
